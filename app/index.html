How a deal earns its verdict

  1. Read the price at the store — or in a deals source that names the store — on 27 Nov 2026. The source and its date sit on every card.
  2. Check the maths — the discount on each card is computed from the two prices shown. If they do not add up, the deal does not ship.
  3. Ask if it is really a deal — a new low, a strong price, a modest cut, or a routine sale. A small cut on a product that sold cheaper months ago says so on its card.
  4. Compare across stores — where the same product is cheaper elsewhere, the compare table above says so.

Prices move fast in Black Friday week and can sell out. The price on the store page at checkout is the final word — if it has moved, the card’s “checked” date tells you how fresh our reading is.
